About

I offer individual coaching and men’s groups, helping people reclaim their vitality and reason for being alive.

One of my mentors says that healing is being able to relax in the presence of another. Simple, not easy. When we’re truly relaxed we contact our feelings, intuitions, and particular genius. The fire inside us burns brightly. I make space for this beautiful and at times rigorous journey with my heart, presence, and willingness to look directly at life.

The impact of our work will ripple out through your relationships: to yourself, to friends and lovers, and to your work.

Approach

The theory behind the coaching

R.D. Laing said there are basically two kinds of therapy. In one, you’re an object to be fixed; in another, you’re a person to be accepted.

Most therapy and coaching is about trying to get you to be different. My style is about building a real heart–to–heart relationship where you can get to know yourself, deeply appreciate yourself, and change because life is naturally evolutionary.

I draw on my training in Hakomi mindfulness–based somatic psychotherapy, mentorship with Bruce Sanguin, profound lived understanding of trauma and recovery, existential philosophy, leadership experience in startups and magazines, and the everyday curriculum of being in a human body.

01

Direct experience

Emotions, sensations, intuitions, your own particular genius. We practice coming back to what you actually feel, in your body, in the present moment.

02

Authentic relationship

Being met directly by another human being without an agenda is what makes the rest possible. It’s also the training ground for all your other relationships—husband or wife, business partners, friends.

03

Freedom

Most of us created a false self to survive childhood or adapt to the conditions of our world. When we wake up to our freedom, there is often grief for the ways we’ve lived. This is good news, and I will help you navigate it.

04

Practices for life

For those who want it, I help you create practices and routines to connect to your natural vitality and come back to life.
